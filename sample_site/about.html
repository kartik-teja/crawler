Who We Are

A tax firm built on precision and trust

HK Tax LLC was founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves a tax professional who is accurate, transparent, and genuinely on their side — all year, not just in April.

Our Story

Rooted in expertise, grown through trust

HK Tax LLC began with a handful of experienced preparers who were tired of watching people overpay, get lost in jargon, or feel abandoned the moment a return was filed. We set out to build the firm we wished existed — one that treats every client's finances with the same care as its own.

More than fifteen years and thousands of returns later, that principle hasn't changed. We've grown into a full-service tax, accounting, and advisory practice serving individuals and businesses across the country — but we still measure success one relationship at a time.

The providence tree is known for its strength, longevity, and resilience. It's the standard we hold ourselves to: dependable through every season, and built to last.

Our Mission

To make expert tax and accounting accessible, understandable, and genuinely valuable — helping every client file accurately, pay only what they owe, and make confident financial decisions with a trusted advisor at their side.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted tax and advisory partner in the country — known not for size, but for the precision of our work, the transparency of our pricing, and the strength of the relationships we build with the people we serve.
